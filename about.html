About EasyStockMarkets

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Who we are

EasyStockMarkets.com is an independent website that offers free, easy-to-use investment calculators and simple explanations of how stock markets and mutual funds work.

Our mission

Many people delay investing because the maths and the jargon feel complicated. We build tools that turn a few numbers into a clear picture, so you can plan with confidence.

What we offer

Our SIP calculator and lumpsum calculator estimate how your money may grow over time. They support step-up SIPs, show year-wise growth and work on any phone or computer. All tools are free and need no sign-up.

How our calculators work

We use standard compound-interest formulas. The results depend only on the numbers you enter and are estimates, not promises. We do not store the values you type into the calculators.

Important note

We are not a broker, mutual fund distributor, SEBI-registered investment advisor or research analyst. Please read our Disclaimer.
